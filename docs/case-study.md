# Case study

## Objective
Help a business operator review changing prices and costs in one consistent workflow.

## Approach
Group observations into competitor, labor, materials, and precious-metal categories. Calculate the size of a change, preserve its stated source and date, and flag it for review before action. Apply operational experience to highlight the need for comparable specifications and commercial terms.

## Builder contribution
Federico defined the business intelligence categories, review workflow, and operating questions. The public prototype demonstrates calculations and review using manually supplied data.

## Measurement plan
Track briefing preparation time, verified-source coverage, stale observation rate, false alerts from noncomparable records, and decisions supported. Establish baselines before claiming time or cost savings.
